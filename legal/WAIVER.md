## 1. The risks of strongman competition

I understand that competing in and warming up for strongman events, including overhead pressing (logs, axles, circus dumbbells, kegs), deadlifts, moving events (yokes, farmer's walks, Husafell stones, sandbags, vehicle and harness pulls) and loading events (Atlas stones, kegs, natural stones), involves heavy, awkward and moving implements and maximal physical effort.

I understand that these risks cannot be removed no matter how careful the Promoter, the Venue, the judges or the loaders are. They include, without limitation:
- muscle tears, tendon and ligament ruptures (biceps, patellar, Achilles, rotator cuff), spinal injuries, hernias, fractures, concussions and crush injuries from dropped or shifting implements;
- cardiovascular events, heat illness, blacking out under load, cuts and skin tears;
- equipment failure, rack or yoke pins set at the wrong height, plates or collars slipping, uneven or wet floors and parking surfaces, and mistakes by volunteer loaders, spotters or judges;
- permanent disability and death.

## 2. My fitness and my duty to inspect

I state that I am in good health, have trained for the weights and events of this competition, and have no medical condition that makes my participation unsafe. I am responsible for inspecting the course, the rack and yoke heights and the implements before each attempt, and for telling a judge or the Promoter at once if I believe anything is unsafe. I will follow the rules of the competition and the instructions of the judges and staff.

## 3. Medical treatment

If I am injured or have a medical emergency at the competition, I authorize the Promoter, Venue staff and medical personnel to give first aid and to arrange emergency transport and treatment. I am responsible for all medical, ambulance and hospital costs incurred for me.

## 4. Assumption of risk and release

In exchange for being allowed to register for and compete in **{{EVENT_NAME}}**, I voluntarily accept all risks of participating, known and unknown. On behalf of myself, my heirs, assigns and personal representatives, I **release, waive, discharge and agree not to sue**:
1. **{{PROMOTER_NAME}}** and its owners, officers, event staff, judges, loaders, spotters and volunteers;
2. the owner and operator of **{{VENUE_NAME_AND_ADDRESS}}** and their staff; and
3. **ZeroTacky**, the registration and scoring software provider, and its owner, operator and contributors,

from all liability, claims, demands and causes of action for any loss, damage, injury or death I suffer while participating in, warming up for, traveling to or from, or attending the competition, **including loss caused by the ordinary negligence of any Released Party**, to the fullest extent the law allows.

## 5. Indemnity

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any claim, loss, cost or expense (including reasonable attorney's fees) brought by me or on my behalf, or by any third party, arising from my participation in the competition or from my breach of this agreement, to the fullest extent the law allows.

## 6. Results, name and image

I agree that the Promoter and ZeroTacky may record and publish my name, division, bodyweight class, scores and placings, and photographs and video of me at the competition, on live scoreboards, in competition archives and in promotion of the competition and of ZeroTacky, without payment to me.

## 7. Athletes under 18

An athlete under 18 may not register on their own. If the athlete is under 18, I confirm that I am the athlete's parent or court-appointed legal guardian, that I am completing this registration for the athlete, that I have read this agreement, and that I agree to it both on the athlete's behalf and on my own behalf, including the release in section 4 and the indemnity in section 5 for any claim the athlete may later bring. I understand the Promoter may require my presence and a paper signature at check-in before the athlete competes, and that the Promoter sets the minimum age for this competition. I will give the Promoter my name and a way to reach me.

## 8. Scoring software

I understand that ZeroTacky is software run by the Promoter and its volunteers. Scores, standings, plate-loading calculations and rack-height callouts shown by the software are tools for the Promoter; the Promoter and the judges decide results and are responsible for checking weights and equipment. Scores may be delayed, corrected or lost if a device is offline. I release ZeroTacky from any claim about scoring errors, lost data or placings.

## 9. Electronic acceptance, copies and general terms

By checking the acceptance box and submitting my registration I sign this agreement electronically, and I agree that this has the same effect as a handwritten signature. ZeroTacky records the date and time of my acceptance and an identifier of the exact text I accepted. The current text is available at any time at `zerotacky.com/legal/waiver`; I can print or save it from that page or ask the Promoter for a copy.

I have read this agreement, I understand that I am giving up substantial legal rights by signing it, and I sign it freely. If any part of it is held invalid, the rest stays in force. This agreement is governed by the law of the state where the competition is held, unless the Promoter's own registration terms say otherwise.
