## 1. Acknowledgment of Inherent Risks in Strongman Competition

I understand and acknowledge that competing and warming up in strongman events—including but not limited to overhead pressing (logs, axles, circus dumbbells, kegs), deadlifting, moving events (yokes, farmers walks, husafell stones, sandbags, vehicle/harness pulls), and loading events (Atlas stones, kegs, natural stones)—involves strenuous physical exertion and heavy, awkward, and moving implements.

I understand that participation carries inherent risks that cannot be eliminated regardless of the care taken by the Promoter, Venue, judges, or spotters/loaders. These risks include, without limitation:
- Muscle tears, tendon or ligament ruptures (including biceps, patellar, Achilles, and rotator cuff injuries), spinal injuries, hernias, bone fractures, concussions, and crush injuries from dropped or shifting implements;
- Cardiovascular events, heat exhaustion, syncope (blacking out under load), lacerations, and skin tears;
- Equipment malfunction, rack or yoke pin misadjustment, plate or collar slippage, uneven floor or parking-lot surfaces, and errors by volunteer loaders, spotters, or judges.

## 2. Representation of Physical Fitness and Equipment Inspection

I represent that I am in good physical health, have trained sufficiently for the weights and events of this competition, and have no medical condition that would make my participation unsafe. I agree that it is my responsibility to inspect the course, rack/yoke heights, and implements before my attempt and to immediately notify a judge or the Promoter if I believe any condition is unsafe.

## 3. Consent to Emergency Medical Treatment

In the event of an injury or medical emergency during the competition, I authorize the Promoter, Venue staff, and medical personnel to administer first aid and arrange emergency medical transport or treatment. I agree that I am solely responsible for all medical, ambulance, and hospital costs incurred on my behalf.

## 4. Voluntary Assumption of Risk and Release of Liability

In consideration for being permitted to register and compete in **{{EVENT_NAME}}**, I hereby voluntarily assume all risks of participation and, on behalf of myself, my heirs, assigns, and personal representatives, **RELEASE, WAIVE, DISCHARGE, AND COVENANT NOT TO SUE**:
1. **{{PROMOTER_NAME}}**, its directors, event staff, judges, loaders, and volunteers;
2. The owner and operator of **{{VENUE_NAME_AND_ADDRESS}}**; and
3. **ZeroTacky** (the registration and scoring software provider) and its operator,

from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action arising out of or related to any loss, damage, injury, or death that I may sustain while participating in, warming up for, or spectating at the competition, whether caused by the **ordinary negligence** of the Released Parties or otherwise, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

## 5. Media and Results Release

I grant permission to the Promoter and ZeroTacky to record and publish my name, division, competition scores, overall placements, and event photographs or video footage for live scoreboards, historical competition archives, and event promotion without compensation.

## 6. Severability and Electronic Acceptance

If any portion of this Waiver is held invalid or unenforceable under applicable law, the remainder shall continue in full legal force and effect. By checking the acceptance box and submitting my registration electronically, I intend my electronic acceptance to have the same legal effect as a handwritten signature.
