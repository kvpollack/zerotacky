## What to look at
1. **The guide against the product.** Walk `/how-it-works/first-show` with the staging guide meet (or a new one of your own) open in another tab. Every sentence that describes a control, a default or a consequence should be true on the current build: field names, which tab a thing is on, what Save Changes vs Save Listing Settings commit, what Accept does to the roster and lot number, what rotating the join code does to a signed-in phone, what the capacity line counts. Flag anything the screenshots show that the text does not explain, and anything the text promises that the screen does not do.
2. **"On the public side" claims.** For each step, check the stated public effect on `/shows`, `/shows/<slug>`, `/register/<slug>` and `/m/<slug>` with the guide meet: division chips, implement chips, target weights per division on the registration page, roster names on the scoreboard before any scores, the Up Now strips per station, the capacity line after an Accept.
3. **FAQ accuracy.** Sixteen answers; hold each to the build. Particular ones: no account needed to register (and what signing in does), 12+ and under-18 guardian rule, what is public about an athlete, join code vs admin PIN, roster two ways, unlist vs delete (a live or completed meet cannot be deleted), claim-a-gym flow. Links in answers must land on the route they name.
4. **Publish key removal, both states, phone scorer.** Signed out on `/scorer` Meet Setup > Advanced: no key input anywhere, Publish disabled, the Sign in link works and returns you. Signed in: Publishing as you, Publish works, no claim UI. Then the legacy path: put a value in `localStorage.ZEROTACKY_PUBLISH_KEY_V1`, reload signed in; the Forget line appears and clears it; reload signed out and confirm the saved key does not re-enable Publish. Joined volunteer phones (join code) are unaffected; check Judge and Pit Crew roles still write.
5. **Nothing else mentions the key.** Grep the rendered pages (`/how-it-works`, `/website/account`, `/scorer`, `/join`) for "publish key" or "promoter key". Expect none.
6. **Static page hygiene.** `/how-it-works/first-show` canonical, title, images lazy-load with alt text, anchor nav lands on each step, Back link, mobile width (images stack, no horizontal scroll). `/how-it-works#roadmap` and `#faq` anchors. 7. **Copy rules** as before: no em-dashes, no real meet or venue names (the test checks `All Out Strongfest`, `Power Plant`, `Pollack`), no promise of a feature the roadmap lists as coming.
8. **Known, not claimed:** no video yet (promised after the first show); `public/guide/02-my-meets.png` is in the repo but not referenced; QR codes in the Meet Day screenshot encode localhost (visually identical, not scannable to anything useful); the server still accepts `p_key`.
