## What to look at, in priority order
1. **010 (`supabase/010_meet_lifecycle.sql`), applied to prod.** (a) `zt_publish_meet` on conflict: `is_demo = meets.is_demo and excluded.is_demo`. Confirm a real meet cannot be flipped back to demo by any publish payload, and that a demo meet becomes real when the phone sends `isDemo: false`. (b) `zt_delete_meet`: owner only, refuses live and completed, name match is case and whitespace insensitive, cascades through the 004 result-delete guard via a transaction-local `set_config`. Try it as a member who is not the owner, as a keyed phone, and with a name that matches another meet of the same owner. (c) `meet_follows` RLS and `zt_follow_meet`: can a user follow an unlisted meet, a demo meet, or write a row for another user? (d) `zt_my_athlete_meets`: one row per meet, registration wins over follow, `following` flag. Check it never leaks another user's registration and that a withdrawn registration still shows as the user's.
2. **/join role landing (`app/join/JoinClient.tsx`).** Judge and Promoter require the PIN via `zt_check_admin_pin` before the role is stored. Confirm there is no client-side path to the judge pad without the PIN (BottomNav, stored `ROLE_STORAGE_KEY`, direct URL). Spectator is read-only PitCrew: no pin override, bar toggle frozen, custom weight hidden. Try to write a score as spectator.
3. **Password reset (`lib/auth/useAuth.ts`, `components/website/AccountClient.tsx`).** `resetPasswordForEmail` redirects to `/website/account`; recovery is detected by the `PASSWORD_RECOVERY` event or `type=recovery` in the hash. Walk the real email once with your staging address (nobody has yet). Check the recovery panel cannot be reached without a recovery session, that `updateUser` is refused when signed out, and whether the signed-in "Change password" should ask for the current password (it does not; say whether that is acceptable for this audience).
4. **Athlete My Meets and Follow (`components/promoter/AthleteMeetsSection.tsx`, `components/website/FollowShowButton.tsx`, `lib/promoter/rules.ts` `bucketAthleteMeets`/`athleteMeetBadge`).** Buckets and badges against each registration status. The section renders for every signed-in user on `/promoter`; confirm nothing promoter-only is reachable from it for a non-promoter.
5. **Demo boards (`app/m/[slug]/ScoreboardClient.tsx`, `lib/website/data.ts` `getDemoScoreboardState`).** A demo slug renders fixture data with the DEMO badge and skips polling. Confirm a real slug never falls into the demo path (e.g. a real meet whose slug collides with a fixture), and that `NEXT_PUBLIC_ZT_DEMO_SHOWS=0` hides both the shows and the boards.
6. **Stations step (`components/setup/builder/StationsSection.jsx`).** Division `flight` must always be a live station id after add, remove and reassign; removing a station with divisions on it must leave them visibly unassigned, not silently on a dead id. Dashboard tab numbering changed (Meet Day is 8): grep for stale "tab 7" references in copy.
7. **New surface (`app/how-it-works`, `app/*/opengraph-image.tsx`, `components/promoter/MeetDayQrCards.tsx`).** The OG image routes take a slug from the URL and render it into an image: check for anything the renderer would choke on (very long slugs, unicode, HTML). The QR print window uses `document.write` with escaped strings; confirm the escaping covers meet names. `qrcode@1.5.4` is a new dependency; note anything you know against it.
8. **Copy pass.** Kurt's rule from tonight: customer-facing text must say what a feature does for the person, not how it is built, and no colon-then-fragments sentences. Flag any you find in `lib/website/howItWorks.ts`, the Account page, the Stations step and the run order and tiebreaker explainers (`RUN_ORDER_OPTIONS`, `TIEBREAK_OPTIONS` in `MeetSection.jsx`; the explainers must match `lib/engine/runOrder.ts` and `scoring.ts` exactly).
